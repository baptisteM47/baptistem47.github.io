# Cost-benefit analysis of the NH1 tidal farm

Cost-benefit analysis, M2, 2025-11. With Victoria Godio, Raphaël Perilhou, Toulouse School of Economics.

**77%** probability of a negative NPV (realistic scenario)

## Question

Is France's first pilot tidal farm (Raz Blanchard, €90M investment) socially efficient over 25 years?

## Method

Benefits: electricity sales and avoided CO₂ emissions, jobs through a shadow wage. Costs: CAPEX and OPEX. 2.75% discount rate, Monte Carlo simulation on the electricity price and three emissions scenarios.

## Results

- Probability of a negative NPV between 70% and 84% depending on the scenario
- NPV is highly sensitive to the electricity resale price
- Learning gains and future cost reductions fall outside the 25-year horizon
