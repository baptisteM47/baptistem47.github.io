# Government spending and activity: a SVAR analysis

Macroeconomics of risk, M2, 2026-01. With Victoria Godio, Raphaël Perilhou, Toulouse School of Economics.

**1947–2015** quarterly U.S. data

## Question

How does a government spending shock pass through to GDP, consumption and economic policy uncertainty in the United States?

## Method

Unit root and cointegration tests (Johansen), Blanchard and Perotti detrending, four-variable SVAR identified by Cholesky, impulse responses and variance decomposition over six years.

## Results

- GDP responds positively in the short run, then negatively at longer horizons
- Consumption responds weakly and negatively
- Economic policy uncertainty responds countercyclically, but only slightly
