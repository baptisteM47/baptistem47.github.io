# Anxiety, depression and socio-economic factors in the U.S.

Empirical project, M1, 2025-03. With Victoria Godio, Maëlle Tchoukriel-Eyraud, Toulouse School of Economics.

**15.6M** observations (MH-CLD, 2018–2022)

## Question

Which demographic and socio-economic factors explain the prevalence of anxiety and depression in the United States?

## Method

SAMHSA's MH-CLD dataset, probit models with robust standard errors.

## Results

- Contrary to common belief, more educated people in more demanding jobs are more affected
- The study highlights the hidden costs of work stress and academic pressure
