# Extreme values: the size of U.S. cities

Extreme risk analysis, M2, 2026-04. With Victoria Godio, Raphaël Perilhou, Toulouse School of Economics.

**γ ≈ 0.74** tail index, 788 cities

## Question

Does U.S. city population follow a heavy-tailed law, is the tail stable over time and across regions, and what can we expect for 2040?

## Method

Census data 2010–2019. Hill and bias-reduced Hill estimators, diagnostic QQ-plots, Weissman extreme quantiles by region, and projection of city sizes to 2040.

## Results

- The tail index is very stable from 2010 to 2019: the heavy tail persists
- Every region is heavy-tailed, but with strong heterogeneity (New York distorts the Northeast)
- Extreme value theory estimates extreme quantiles better than the empirical quantile
