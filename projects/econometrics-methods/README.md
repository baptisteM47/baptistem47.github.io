# VaR backtesting, Heckman model and GMM

Econometric methods, M2, 2026-01. With Victoria Godio, Toulouse School of Economics.

**5** VaR methods backtested

## Question

Three applied exercises: correct a selection bias, compare Value at Risk models, estimate supply and demand by GMM.

## Method

Censored model and Heckman correction with robustness checks. Historical, Gaussian, standardized Student, extreme value and historical-on-returns VaR, then coverage tests. GMM estimation of a supply and demand system with instruments.

## Results

- Five VaR approaches compared on exceedances and coverage tests
- Selection bias tested across several specifications: significant in some, not in others
- Supply and demand coefficients identified with instrumental variables
